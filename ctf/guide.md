## :zap: How we play this CTF

- `/add-category web, crypto, …` here in this channel creates a channel per challenge category.
- `/create-challenge <name>` in a category channel starts a thread for that challenge, or adds you to it when it already exists.
- `/solved <flag>` in a challenge thread marks the challenge as solved.
- The pinned challenge overview in this channel lists every challenge, with links to their threads.
- The **Leave** button below this message takes you out of the CTF.

For anything else, ask a moderator.
